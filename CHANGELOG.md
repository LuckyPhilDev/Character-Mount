## [1.15.0] - 2026-10-05

### Added
- **Ground mount key** Choose whether Alt, Ctrl, Shift or no key forces a ground mount when you press the mount macro, in the Macros settings.
- **Key bindings** Summon mount and Summon ground mount appear under Lucky's Character Mount in the game's Key Bindings, so you can mount from a key without placing a macro.

### Fixed
- A mount restored from the Excluded list keeps its Racial, Class or other source label instead of coming back as Manual.
- Fixed an error with the settings panel.

## [1.14.0] - 2026-09-15

### Added
- **Other Char** A button on the New Mount Unlocked popup that lists your other characters in their class colours. Tick the ones you want and the mount is added to each of their lists.

### Fixed
- Learning several mounts back to back now shows the New Mount Unlocked popup for each one in turn, not only the last. (Thanks for the report Tuulani)
