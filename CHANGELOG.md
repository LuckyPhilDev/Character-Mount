## [1.13.0] - 2026-09-03

### Added
- **Total remember time** A slider under Remember rides shorter than in Preferences, capping how long one mount keeps coming back. Hop off and re-summon as often as you like inside it, then the next summon rolls something new. (Thanks for the suggestion Tuulani)

### Improved
- **Remember rides shorter than** The Preferences slider previously called Stay on the same mount. The seconds you set have always been the length of ride it remembers, not a time to stay put, and the name now says so. Your existing value is kept.
