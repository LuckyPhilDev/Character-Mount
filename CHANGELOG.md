## [1.11.2] - 2026-08-21

### Improved
- **Excluded view** The mount list window shows one list at a time. Click the Excluded line above the buttons to switch to your excluded mounts, and click again to come back. (Thanks for the report BanditC64, and the suggestion Tuulani)

## [1.11.1] - 2026-08-20

### Improved
- **Under the hood** A tidy-up of the addon's internals. Nothing changes in how it looks or plays.

## [1.11.0] - 2026-08-19

### Improved
- **Lucky's Utils bundled** The shared library now ships inside the addon, so there is no separate CurseForge download. If you have the standalone Lucky's Utils installed you can remove it, as long as no other Lucky addon still needs it.
